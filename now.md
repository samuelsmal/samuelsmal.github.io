# What I'm doing now

## Consulting, aka earning money

My day-job at [D-ONE](https://d-one.ai) exposes me to many challenges and roles. Most of the time I
am a tech lead, sometimes I am just a data analyst or a software engineer.

Currently my time is ~~divided~~ focused into ~~multiple~~ one thing ~~projects~~: Taking care of my
little daughter until the middle of October full time.

Then back to work!

1. Building a real-time data processing system for a medical device manufacturer, which will allow
   them to make low latency, in-process, agentic decisions based on sensor data in the future.
   Supervising the ML-part, coming up with a good architecture, writing new cloud-native
   guidelines for the client... One of my favourite projects in a while.
2. Supporting the company with acquiring new projects and clients. That means mostly creating
   architectures, answering RfPs, visiting and presenting the company to prospective clients.

In addition to that I am advising multiple friends of mine from time to time on their startups. It's
a bit more defined than just a chat among friends. Mostly on team composition, hiring and
architecture.

## Fermenting food

Roughly around 2012, when I realised that I could not figure out if the yoghurt had spoiled, I
decided to train my body to smell and taste food. Learning about fermentation has since been a
source of inspiration for me. I did a lot of kombucha, sourdough bread (still going strong, Katja
II is now more than six years old!) and chili sauce. It is the chili sauce that I want to create a
business around. Just for the kicks.

## Tea

My parents gave me my love for tea. Since 2024 I am a member of the board of the [Swiss Tea
Club](https://teeclub.ch). A highly prestigious job.

## Doing lots of sport

After my hip surgery I am finally back to feeling healthy and fit. So I try to do as many hikes,
bike rides, camping trips, cross-country skiing days, ... as I can. Very Swiss. Also going back to
my old Shotokan Karate Dojo and seeing how my old pupils are now much better than I could ever be
brings me lots of joy. Couldn't be more proud.

## Being a father

Probably, after my hip replacement, the biggest change in my life over the last few years. By far
the most challenging task. Never before have I felt so helpless and powerful at the same time. My
girlfriend and I hold no hard power, only soft. Trying to support the two teenagers, with their
baggage, hopes, fears and dreams, is a source of focus on what really matters.

Now, since September 2025, I am also a father of a daughter. Oh boy, did this give another push
for changing my priorities. Listening to [How Other Dads Dad](https://howotherdadsdad.com/) is a
blast and highly recommended.
